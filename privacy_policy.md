# Privacy Policy for QRStudio

**Effective Date: July 26, 2026**

QRStudio ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our QR code generation services.

## 1. Data Collection and Usage
QRStudio is designed to be a privacy-first application. 
*   **Local Processing:** All data you enter into the app to generate QR codes (including but not limited to URLs, UPI IDs, Wi-Fi credentials, location coordinates, contact details, and social media handles) is processed **locally on your device**. 
*   **No Data Transmission:** We do not collect, store, or transmit any of the data you enter to our servers or any third-party services.
*   **Generated QR Codes:** The images generated by the App are created and stored on your device only when you choose to save or share them.

## 2. Device Permissions
The App may request the following permissions to provide its features:
*   **Storage (Read/Write):** Required to save generated QR code images to your device's gallery and to allow you to upload custom logos from your storage to be included in your QR codes.
*   **Location (Optional):** If you use the "Location QR" feature, the app may request location access to help you find your current coordinates. This data is used only for the purpose of generating the QR code and is not stored or shared by us.

## 3. Third-Party Services
The App does not use any third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking tools. We do not share any information with third parties.

## 4. Security
Since all data remains on your device, the security of your information depends on the security of your mobile device. We recommend using standard device security features (like screen locks) to protect your data.

## 5. Changes to This Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Effective Date" at the top.
